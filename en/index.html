About us

Japanese craft in Håkonsgaten

Sushi Roll Sentrum is right in the middle of Bergen and the place for fresh, flavourful sushi on the go. We make sashimi, nigiri and creative maki rolls from scratch with quality ingredients, ready for pickup or delivery the same day.

We want every order to taste like you were sitting in a restaurant, even when you eat it at home, at the office or in your student flat. That is why we focus on fresh fish, good rice and a menu that suits both new guests and regular sushi lovers.
